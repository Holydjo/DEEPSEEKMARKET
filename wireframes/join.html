1Seven steps, one decision each

Account, business, services, photos, location/area, hours, review. Each step is independently validatable and saveable. Providers can move back without losing data; the rail shows exactly what remains.

2Service settings drive discovery

“At my home studio / salon / shop / I travel” are not profile decoration: they become search filters and are shown on cards. Selecting none blocks submission because customers cannot plan around an unknown setting.

3Public vs private location

City and neighbourhood are public. Street address is collected for verification and only shared after a request is accepted. The preview never shows a street address, and the map uses an approximate area.

4Draft autosave & return

Every field is saved on blur and every 20 seconds. “Save & exit” emails a resume link. A returning provider lands on the first incomplete step with a “Welcome back” note, not at the beginning.

5Validation is visible before submit

Character minimums, required service, at least one photo, and at least one opening interval are surfaced inline. The “To submit” checklist updates live. Submit is disabled with a reason, never silently.

6Human approval, explained

Step 7 shows a plain-language checklist and the target review time. Submitting sets status PENDING; the dashboard and email confirm receipt. Admin can reject with a note; the provider fixes and resubmits.

7Account is step 1, not a wall

Email, password, name and phone create the user and a draft provider in one transaction. OAuth can be added later. If the email exists, the provider is offered login and draft recovery instead of a dead end.

8Post-submit states

  • Pending: private preview, expected review time, withdraw option.
  • Approved: profile live, share link, first-request guidance.
  • Rejected: reviewer note, highlighted fields, resubmit.
  • Suspended: reason, appeal contact, requests paused.

AMobile wizard rules

One step per screen, progress bar always visible, sticky Back/Save/Continue bar. Native selects and date inputs are used where possible. Long text areas auto-grow and never trap the keyboard.

BPhotos will be the hardest step on mobile

Offer camera + library, client-side crop, max 8 photos, 5 MB each, and written guidance (“well-lit, your actual work, no stock images”). Upload progress and retry are explicit.

CApproval is a feature, not friction

The pending screen is reassuring and gives a concrete timeframe. It sets expectations that editing core identity/business data may trigger a short re-review, while services/hours changes go live immediately for approved providers.

DAccessibility & trust

Labels are always visible (not placeholder-only), errors announce with `aria-live`, focus moves to the first invalid field, and the progress bar has a text equivalent. Uploaded photos require alt text or a short description.

Open decision: should account creation support Google/Apple sign-in in v1? I propose email + password only for launch, with OAuth in a later milestone, to keep the admin/identity model simple.
